Delete Your Account

FnT Tracker  ·  Account & Data Deletion

Easiest way: Open the FnT Tracker app → go to the Account tab → scroll to the bottom → tap Delete Account. Your data will be deleted immediately.
Warning: Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be removed.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed from our servers:

  • All trip entries (earnings, fuel, expenses, notes)
  • All driver records
  • All vehicle records
  • All booking and customer records
  • All loan records
  • Your profile information and photo
  • Your Google Sign-In account link

Data is deleted within 30 days of your request. Some data may be retained in backups for up to 30 days before being permanently purged.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us. Please include the email address associated with your Google account.

Email Us to Delete Account

We will confirm deletion within 30 days.
